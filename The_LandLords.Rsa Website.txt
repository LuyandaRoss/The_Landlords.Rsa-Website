The_LandLords.Rsa Website

Project Overview

The_LandLords.Rsa is a streetwear fashion brand website developed as part of the WEDE5020 Web Development Portfolio of Evidence (POE).

The website aims to establish an online presence for the brand by showcasing its clothing collections, providing information about the business, and allowing customers to submit product enquiries.

The brand focuses on teenagers and young adults who are interested in streetwear culture, Y2K fashion, basketball-inspired clothing, and self-expression through fashion.

Slogan:
"RENT IS DUE"

Website Purpose

The purpose of the website is to:

- Promote The_LandLords.Rsa brand.
- Showcase clothing collections.
- Provide information about the company.
- Allow customers to submit enquiries.
- Increase brand awareness.
- Create a foundation for future e-commerce functionality.

Target Audience

The website is intended for:

- Teenagers
- Young Adults
- Streetwear Enthusiasts
- Fashion-Conscious Consumers
- Basketball Culture Enthusiasts

Website Pages

Home Page
Introduces visitors to the brand and showcases featured collections and new product releases.

About Page
Provides information about the history, mission, vision, and values of The_LandLords.Rsa.

Shop Page
Displays available collections and products offered by the brand.

Enquiry Page
Allows customers to submit enquiries regarding products, sizes, pricing, and availability.

Contact Page
Provides contact details and social media links for customer communication.
