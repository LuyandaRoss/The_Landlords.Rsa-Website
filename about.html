Our story

From a personal vision to a street uniform.

The_LandLords.Rsa was established in 2024 from a passion for clothing design, culture and self-expression. What began as a creative project became a growing brand with a clear point of view: style should feel like ownership.

We create pieces with Y2K energy, premium attitude and an unmistakable South African identity.
