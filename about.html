About Us

Our Story

The_LandLords.Rsa is a South African streetwear brand established in 2024. The brand was created from a passion for fashion, creativity and self-expression.

The founder developed an interest in clothing design and decided to share original ideas with the public. What started as a personal creative project evolved into a growing streetwear brand focused on confidence and individuality.

Mission Statement

To create stylish and expressive streetwear that allows young people to showcase their individuality and confidence through fashion.

Vision Statement

To become a recognised South African streetwear brand that inspires creativity, confidence and self-expression.

Brand Values
